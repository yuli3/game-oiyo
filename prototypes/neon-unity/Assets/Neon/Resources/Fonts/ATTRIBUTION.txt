The bundled derivatives were statically instantiated at weight 400 and subset to the
Unicode text literals used by this game, then renamed to “OIYO Neon Korean”,
“OIYO Neon Japanese” and “OIYO Neon Chinese SC” to comply with the OFL reserved-name rule.
